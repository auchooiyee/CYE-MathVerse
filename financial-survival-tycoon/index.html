FORMULA CHEAT SHEET (FORM 4)

CHAPTER 10 • FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT
1. Net Cash Flow:
\(\text{Net Cash Flow} = \text{Total Income} - \text{Total Expenses}\)

Positive: surplus savings; Negative: cash deficit leading to high-interest debt.

2. Net Worth:
\(\text{Net Worth} = \text{Total Assets} - \text{Total Liabilities}\)
3. Compound Interest Formula:
\(A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}\)

\(P\) = principal, \(r\) = annual interest rate, \(n\) = compounding periods per year, \(t\) = years.

4. Return on Investment (ROI):
\(\text{ROI} = \frac{\text{Total Return}}{\text{Initial Investment}} \times 100\%\)
5. Emergency Fund Rule:

Must equal 3 to 6 months of fixed & variable living expenses.

CHAPTER 9 • PROBABILITY OF COMBINED EVENTS
1. Single Event Probability & Complement:
\(P(A) = \frac{n(A)}{n(S)}, \quad P(A') = 1 - P(A)\)
2. Independent Events (Multiplication Rule):
\(P(A \cap B) = P(A) \times P(B)\)

Occurrence of Event A does not affect the probability of Event B.

3. Dependent Events (Conditional Probability):
\(P(A \cap B) = P(A) \times P(B|A)\)
4. Mutually Exclusive Events (Addition Rule):
\(P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B)\)

Events cannot occur at the same time: \(P(A \cap B) = 0\).

5. Non-Mutually Exclusive Events:
\(P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)\)
