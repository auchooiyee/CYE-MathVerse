📖 Rules & Tax Guide

Welcome to Salary & Tax Tycoon, an authentic adulting and wealth building simulation!

Game Phases Every Year:

  1. Wheel of Fortune: Spin for salary bonuses, jackpot opportunities, or audit risks.
  2. Payday: Stamp your payslip (tap or drag stamp) to collect your salary.
  3. Marketplace: Purchase Insurance for protection & LHDN Tax Reliefs to cut taxes.
  4. Life Events: Draw real-life event cards (medical emergencies, promotions, bonuses).
  5. LHDN Income Tax: Calculate Chargeable Income and progressive income tax.
  6. Investments: Allocate savings across Fixed Deposits, ETFs, Real Estate REITs, and Stocks.
  7. Year-End Summary: Review savings growth and financial performance!
